What is this and how to help collect prices

Free Market prices of scrolls (and other items) in Old School Maple (MapleStory Worlds), collected by a small tracker that screenshots the shop window while you browse hired merchants and reads the item names and prices. It does not touch the game: no memory reading, no clicking, no packets. Screenshot plus OCR, nothing else.

To contribute prices:

  1. Download the tracker (Windows, one exe, no install) and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Run the game at 1920x1080, windowed or full screen. Other window sizes work too, but the game shrinks its UI with the window and small windows read worse.
  3. Run osmsfm-tracker.exe. Windows SmartScreen will warn about an unknown publisher the first time: click "More info", then "Run anyway". Or right-click the exe, Properties, "Unblock" before running.
  4. Press F9 to start collecting, walk through the FM shops, press F9 again to stop. Ctrl+F9 quits. The cursor may rest on the shop window; the row under it is skipped and picked up in the next frame. If the console keeps saying 0 shops, press F10 with a shop open: it saves what the tracker sees to data\debug\, send that file.

Data is uploaded every 5 minutes while collecting and once more when you stop. It is anonymous: the tracker signs its uploads with a random id like anon-3f9c2a. What is collected: item name, price, whether the offer is sold out (greyed), the shop owner's name, shop title, map and Free Market room, the shop's remaining time and a small crop of the row for verification. Nothing about your character, account or chat.

itemn liveask min nowask med 7d sold med 7dsold max 7d buy up tosell atlast seen

Ask = listed (active) prices, sold = greyed-out rows, i.e. prices somebody actually paid; the cheapest go first, so treat sold as a floor. "Live" = offers whose shop is still up (from the shop's timer) and which were not seen sold later; expired ones stay in history. "Buy up to" = lower quartile of asks from the last 7 days, "sell at" = between the sold median and the lowest live ask. A heuristic, not gospel. Names with "?" have a truncated stat or percent. Click a row to see the observations and crops.
